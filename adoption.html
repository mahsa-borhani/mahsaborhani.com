Wholesale distributor · 2022–2025

Every salesperson on board, none reporting to me

Daily use

A new platform is only worth something if people use it. By my final year, every salesperson in a company of more than 450 used ours daily, including for phone orders, and none of them reported to me.

The commercial problem

A new commerce platform, rolled out across 19 branches and four distribution centres. The people who had to change how they worked were branch staff and salespeople with no reporting line to me, and an unused platform is pure cost.

What I did

  • Chaired an e-commerce committee of 11 branch representatives, so every branch had a voice and an owner.
  • Trained local e-commerce champions who could help their own colleagues.
  • Built the toolkits, templates, training and standard procedures each location used.
  • Listened to objections and fixed them.
  • Every year, we recognised the branches with the most online sales.

The result

By my final year, every salesperson in the company used the platform daily, including for phone orders.

What I learned

Make people co-owners, not subjects.

Skills in play

  • Change management
  • Influence without authority
  • Training and enablement
  • Stakeholder management
