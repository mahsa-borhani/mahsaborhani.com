Selling inside AI assistants

98–100%

People now ask AI assistants what to buy. I moved our catalogue into agentic commerce early: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Gemini carry 98–100% of our products, and by November Meta AI and Shop as well.

  • ChannelsChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, Meta AI, Shop
  • CatalogueAbout 480 published products; 1,600+ listings with variants

The commercial problem

Search is moving from links to answers. If an assistant can’t read a product correctly, it can’t recommend it.

What I did

  • Put the catalogue live in agentic commerce: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Gemini, then Meta AI and Shop.
  • Rebuilt our product structured data so Google and AI assistants read every product correctly.
  • Built for AI search in phases: a strategy, then llms.txt with Organization and FAQ structured data, then a measurement framework.

The measurement discipline

The attribution dashboard credited ChatGPT with far more revenue than it earned, because any-click attribution hands the whole journey to ChatGPT if it appears anywhere in it. Checked order by order it was far smaller, so I report the channel order by order: ChatGPT appears in the buying journey of five 2026 orders, four of them paid.

What I learned

I trust the order table over the dashboard.

Skills in play

  • AEO and GEO
  • Structured data
  • Agentic commerce
  • Attribution
  • Shopify

How it’s measured
