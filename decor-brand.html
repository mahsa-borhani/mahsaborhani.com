Home décor brand · 2016–2022

Four times bigger, sold on pictures

4.15×

Rugs, fine art and handcrafted décor: the pieces people buy to finish a room. Over six years I grew revenue 4.15×, with about 80% of sales going to the US, and rose from sales specialist to Vice President of Sales and Operations.

The commercial problem

A high-value, emotional purchase that people research for a long time, sold online to buyers who could not touch the product. Imagery, listing accuracy, reviews and storytelling decided whether a piece sold, across five storefronts and several countries.

What I did

  • Directed the visual content the storefronts sold on: I set the ideas for our photographers, video editors and 3D (3ds Max) artists, then approved, sent back or discarded their work.
  • Ran Meta advertising as a core acquisition channel for more than three years, hands-on: audience building, creative testing and budget allocation, alongside Google, Pinterest, Reddit and pre-auction events.
  • Ran the Amazon channel in Seller Central and FBA, hands-on at first and then through my team: listings and the inventory sent into FBA.
  • Gave each market its own creative, pricing and channel mix.
  • Opened the company’s first wholesale accounts, in the US, Japan and Italy: the B2B side of a consumer brand. I found the first one myself, on Alibaba, and it became a repeat, key account.

A moment I remember

Around 2016, one of our Facebook videos earned a very high relevance score. The auction rewarded it with a falling cost per click and rising delivery, week after week.

The result

Revenue grew 4.15× in six years. I owned the P&L, led a team of 14 across marketing, sales and operations, and was promoted from Sales Specialist to Ecommerce Marketing Director to Vice President, while finishing my MBA. Fourteen months in, the founders moved their attention to their other businesses and left this one with me.

Alongside, from 2016 to 2022, I advised consumer, packaging and pharmaceutical startups as an independent marketing consultant: brand positioning, go-to-market and digital acquisition, across several concurrent clients.

What I learned

Creative quality isn’t a brand nicety. It’s a media discount.

And on leading a team of 14 for the first time, in my own words: “It was hard, but I became a good leader.”
