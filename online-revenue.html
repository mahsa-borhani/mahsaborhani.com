The commercial problem

A distributor selling to trade customers from 19 branches and four distribution centres: by phone, at the counter and online. The job was to grow the online channel, move the commerce platform to its next generation without disrupting trade, and make the site useful to every branch.

What I did

  • Ran Optimizely Configured Commerce in production on Classic, then owned the migration to Spire end to end: requirements, scope, QA and go-live. I directed two implementation partners alongside my own team and stakeholders in IT, Sales, Merchandising and Procurement, and ran Spire through to my departure.
  • Worked with IT on the platform’s cloud hosting, including data-residency questions, and ran delivery in Agile and Scrum: sprint planning, daily stand-ups and backlog management.
  • Rolled out product information management (Inriver/Akeneo) across 20,000+ products: taxonomy, attribute standards, completeness rules, validation workflows, and the governance to keep them true after go-live.
  • Grew organic search with technical SEO, content and site architecture.
  • Turned the printed welcome package into a personal web page for every new customer, with coupon codes, how-to guides and an introduction to the company, and gave every one of the 19 branches its own page.
  • Owned the department’s P&L and the migration-year budget: vendor selection, contract negotiation, purchase orders and agency accountability. Led a team of 9 — 3 direct reports and 6 indirect — across marketing, digital, development, analytics and support.

The result

Online revenue up 93% in three years. Organic sessions up 89% and organic clicks up 101%.

And a hidden return: the site became the sales team’s tool.

What I learned

A rep with two years could answer like a rep with ten.
